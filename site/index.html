Works with Claude CodeChromegit

Step 1

Claude Code finishes

A hook tells Tess the moment a task is done. Or she tests after every commit, or when you press Test. No test scripts to write.

Step 2

Tess uses your app

She reads the change, plans what could break, and clicks through it in a real Chrome while you watch. She can look, but she can’t edit your code.

Step 3

You get one answer

Each check passed, broke, or couldn’t run, with a screenshot and what she saw. She never calls a change safe with a gap.

Step 4

It goes back to Claude Code

When something breaks, this is what Claude Code receives: Tess’s steps, what she expected and what she saw. It fixes the change and she tests again, up to three times.
