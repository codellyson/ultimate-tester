Works with Claude Code, Google Chrome and git

1

Tess learns your app once

She reads your repo and drafts how to test it: how it starts, who uses it, and what must never break. You check the plan, sign in once with a test account, and pick when she tests. No test scripts to write.

2

Then she uses it like a person

When Claude Code finishes, she reads the change, plans what could break, and clicks through it in a real Chrome. She can look, but she can’t edit your code.

3

You get one answer, with proof

Each check passed, broke, or couldn’t run, with the page she saw and what she expected. She never calls a change safe while a check is missing.

4

Claude Code fixes what broke

When something breaks, Claude Code gets Tess’s steps, what she expected and what she saw. It fixes the change and she tests again, up to three times.
